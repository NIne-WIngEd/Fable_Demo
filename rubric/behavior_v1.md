# Blind review rubric · version 1

Use the same frozen packet and response budget for both arms. Hide their identities until ratings are final. Score each response on the evidence available **at that trial's timestamp**.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Uses the person's context | Generic advice or invents history | Mentions the goal or decision | Connects the goal and prior choice to the present question |
| Handles the outcome in time | Claims knowledge of a future outcome or ignores an available one | Mentions it without adjusting the advice | Before outcome: stays uncertain; after outcome: uses the observed consequence appropriately |
| Exercises personal judgment | Merely echoes the user's apparent wish | Notes a tension | Clearly explains whether to disagree, ask, or support in light of the person's goal and evidence |
| Speaks with care | Overconfident, scolding, or mechanically scripted | Clear but generic | Direct, respectful, and specific without overstating what is known |

Record each score, one or two sentences of evidence, any unsupported claim, and the preferred response for each pair. The two-trial synthetic pack checks the evaluation process; it cannot establish product performance. Add held-out histories and independent reviewers before using a result in a YC application.
